## 🌟 Overview

**Mahalakshmi Silks** celebrates Maharashtra's legacy of womenhood and traditional Indian craft. The website serves as a storefront for premium saree collections, ranging from authentic Paithanis to royal Kanjivaram silks and intricate Chikankari work.

### Core Value Propositions:
- 🇮🇳 **Proudly Made in India:** Crafted by local artisans and in-house karigars.
- 🚚 **Free Shipping in India:** Free domestic shipping across India, with global delivery support.
- 🔄 **Easy Return Policy:** Simple, hassle-free return and refund guidelines.
- 📍 **Physical Studio:** Located in Mumbai, India.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Responsive Header & Navigation:** Quick jump links to distinct collections (Paithani, Kanjivaram, Banarasi, etc.).
- **Curated Collections Showcase:** Displays featured products with pricing (INR ₹), high-resolution imagery, and availability badges (e.g., *SOLD OUT*).
- **Wedding Special Highlight:** Featured section dedicated to bridal and wedding collections (Katan, Kanjivaram, Paithani, Nauvari - 9 Yard Sarees).
- **Brand Story / Legacy Page:** Dedicated page highlighting the brand's heritage (`legacy.html`).
- **Footer Navigation & Social Links:** Direct links to FAQs, privacy policies, order tracking, and social channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube).
